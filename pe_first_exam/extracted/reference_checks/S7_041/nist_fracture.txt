                                                                            4.         Conclusion

                                                                            There is a strong theoretical foundation for Weibull statisti-
                                                                            cal analysis of strength data based on extreme value theory,
                                                                            fracture mechanics, and demonstrated ﬂaw size distributions.
                                                                            However, awareness of the conditions and limitations inher-
                                                                            ent in Weibull analyses, especially those pertaining to existing
                                                                            ﬂaw populations and quality of data, is important for mean-
                                                                            ingful application and interpretation. A great deal of useful
                                                                            information is available through Weibull analysis. Among
                                                                            the most useful applications are comparisons of strength
                                                                            values and ranges for different stress conﬁgurations, which
                                                                            were demonstrated in Section 3 for several dental materi-
                                                                            als.
