IInfluence of Deoxidation on Sulfide Morphologies and the Machin-
ability of Low Carbon Resulfurized Free-Machining Steel
Atsushi Hattori, Takashi Kano, and Tomoki Hanyuda


Synopsis
  Effect of deoxidation on machinability of low carbon resulfurized steel with 0.5mass% of sulfur content were investi-
gated. Both life of carbide tool steel and roughnesss of machined surface have a negative peak value at the same amount of
oxygen content in these steel. These phenomena primarily depends on behavior of built-up-edge(BUE). Tool life and sur-
face roughness increases as the height of BUE grows up. The height of BUE is affected by sulfide morphology and other
factor such as sulfide composition, fineness of microstructure, which are determined by oxygen content and deoxidiser.
